## Inventory conclusions

- The duplicate global AI revision surface was the only visible control group found to have an unsafe, non-local target; it was removed.
- Assessment and schedule controls with no active downstream feature are now conditionally hidden.
- Intake accepts exactly three meaningful entry paths: a brief, an explicit title, or a readable source.
- All course-editing surfaces converge on the `CourseProject` graph, snapshot history, derived readiness, and export pipeline rather than maintaining parallel copies.
- Remaining external-state controls—Stripe, paid AI, Supabase policies, and Canvas sandbox import—retain explicit loading/error or owner boundaries and are not represented as locally verified.
